# Retail Performance & Customer Retention Dashboard — Tableau Public Build Guide

This guide walks through building the two-dashboard Tableau Public workbook from the pre-aggregated CSVs generated by [`src/export_tableau_data.py`](../../src/export_tableau_data.py).

---

## 1. Data Sources (Small Aggregated CSVs — No 794K-Row Load)

Connect your Tableau Public workbook to these four files (gross purchases only, `is_return = 0`, matching `sql/features.sql`):

| File | Rows | Size | Purpose |
|:---|---:|---:|:---|
| [`outputs/tableau/sales_orders.csv`](sales_orders.csv) | 36,594 | 2.9 MB | Invoice-level purchases for exact KPIs (`COUNTD` customers & orders), YoY monthly revenue trend, and Top 10 Countries |
| [`outputs/tableau/sales_products.csv`](sales_products.csv) | 117,282 | 13 MB | Month × Country × Product revenue with canonical product names (`product_label`) for Top 10 Products |
| [`outputs/decile_table.csv`](../decile_table.csv) | 10 | <1 KB | Decile-level churn validation table (Decile 1 = highest predicted risk) |
| [`outputs/tableau/scored_customers.csv`](scored_customers.csv) | 2,775 | 375 KB | Scored Sept 9, 2011 test-snapshot customers: predicted churn risk, decile, RFM quintiles/segments, last order date, total spend |

### Data Type & Relationship Setup in Tableau
1. In `sales_orders.csv`, ensure `order_date` and `order_month` are **Date** types, `customer_id` is **String** (dimension), and `country` has **Geographic Role → Country/Region** (optional).
2. In `sales_products.csv`, ensure `order_month` is **Date** type and `country` is **String**.
3. **Link `sales_orders` and `sales_products` for shared filters**:
   - Keep them as two data sources in the workbook, then go to **Data → Edit Blend Relationships...**
   - Primary: `sales_orders`, Secondary: `sales_products` → confirm **Custom** mapping links `order_month = order_month` and `country = country`.
   - When adding **Date Range (`order_month`)** and **Country (`country`)** filters on Dashboard 1, set **Apply to Worksheets → All Using Related Data Sources**. Both filters will now update **every** sheet in Dashboard 1 (KPIs, Monthly Trend, Top 10 Countries, and Top 10 Products) consistently.

## 2. Recommended Colour Palette (3–4 Colours Only)

Use one cohesive palette across both dashboards:
- **Primary Navy (`#1F5A9E`)**: 2011 trend line, Top Countries/Products bars, Champions/Low-Risk bars
- **Muted Slate (`#6B7280`)**: 2010 comparison line, below-average deciles, reference lines
- **Alert Coral (`#D95F43`)**: High-risk deciles (above average / Deciles 1–2), At Risk / Hibernating segments, Dec 2011 partial-month marker
- **Light Neutral (`#F3F4F6` / `#E5E7EB`)**: subtle gridlines and KPI card borders

## 3. Dashboard 1: Sales Performance

### Sheet 1A — KPI Tiles (from `sales_orders.csv`)
Create four calculated fields:
- `Total Revenue`: `SUM([order_value])` → format as Currency (`£#,##0`) → **£17,068,568** (all dates)
- `Number of Orders`: `COUNTD([invoice])` → format as Integer (`#,##0`) → **36,594**
- `Active Customers`: `COUNTD([customer_id])` → format as Integer (`#,##0`) → **5,852**
- `Average Order Value`: `SUM([order_value]) / COUNTD([invoice])` → format as Currency (`£#,##0.00`) → **£466.43**

**Layout**: Build either 4 separate single-number sheets placed side-by-side at the top of the dashboard, or 1 sheet using `Measure Names` on Columns and `Measure Values` on Text. Keep titles clean and centered; turn off tooltips or format concisely.

### Sheet 1B — Monthly Revenue Trend: 2010 vs 2011 Overlaid (from `sales_orders.csv`)
- **Sheet Filter**: `year` = `2010, 2011` (keeps the YoY comparison focused on the two full calendar years; Dec 2009 still counts in KPIs/Countries/Products unless filtered out by Date Range).
- **Columns**: `month_name` (Right-click → **Sort → By Field → `month_num` Ascending** so it displays `Jan` → `Dec`).
- **Rows**: `SUM([order_value])` (`£#,##0,,.0"M"` or `£#,##0,"K"`).
- **Marks (Line + Circle dual axis or Line with Shape/Annotation)**:
  - `year` (as Discrete Dimension) on **Color** (`2010` = Muted Slate `#6B7280`, `2011` = Primary Navy `#1F5A9E`).
  - **Mark Dec 2011 as partial**: Add `month_status` to **Detail** and **Shape** (or create a dual-axis dot on `SUM([order_value])` colored by `month_status`, highlighting `Partial (Dec 1-9)` in Alert Coral `#D95F43`), and right-click the Dec 2011 point → **Annotate → Mark**: `"Dec 2011 partial (Dec 1–9 only: £512K)"`.
- **Tooltip**:
  - `Year: <year> | Month: <month_name>`
  - `Revenue: <SUM(order_value)>`
  - `Orders: <COUNTD(invoice)> | Active Customers: <COUNTD(customer_id)>`
  - `Status: <month_status>`

### Sheet 1C — Top 10 Countries by Revenue (from `sales_orders.csv`)
- **Rows**: `country` → Filter to **Top 10 by `SUM(order_value)`** → Sort descending by `SUM(order_value)`.
- **Columns**: `SUM([order_value])` in Primary Navy `#1F5A9E`.
- **Label**: `SUM([order_value])` (or `% of Total Revenue` using Quick Table Calculation).
- **Tooltip**: `Country`, `Revenue`, `% of Total`, `Orders (COUNTD(invoice))`, `Active Customers (COUNTD(customer_id))`.

### Sheet 1D — Top 10 Products by Revenue (from `sales_products.csv`)
- **Rows**: `product_label` → Filter to **Top 10 by `SUM(revenue)`** → Sort descending by `SUM(revenue)`.
- **Columns**: `SUM([revenue])` in Primary Navy `#1F5A9E`.
- **Tooltip**: `Product: <description>`, `Stock Code: <stock_code>`, `Revenue: <SUM(revenue)>`, `Units Sold: <SUM(units_sold)>`, `Orders: <SUM(order_count)>`.

### Dashboard 1 Assembly & Shared Filters
- Place the 4 KPI tiles across the top, Sheet 1B (Monthly Trend) full-width in the middle, and Sheet 1C (Top 10 Countries) + Sheet 1D (Top 10 Products) side-by-side along the bottom.
- Expose two filters on the top-right/header, both set to **Apply to Worksheets → All Using Related Data Sources**:
  1. `order_month` (Continuous Month/Year **Range of Dates** slider, `Dec 2009` – `Dec 2011`)
  2. `country` (**Multiple Values (Dropdown)** with Search & "All" option)

### Verified Insight Caption (Dashboard 1)
> **What the data shows:** Revenue surges in autumn across both years—September through November generate **36.2% of 2010 revenue (£2.97M)** and **37.7% of 2011 revenue (£3.08M)**, peaking in November (**£1.16M** in 2010 and **£1.14M** in 2011)—while the **United Kingdom accounts for 83.7% (£14.29M)** of total sales (*Dec 2011 includes Dec 1–9 only*).

## 4. Dashboard 2: Customer Retention

### Sheet 2A — Churn Rate by Risk Decile (from `outputs/decile_table.csv`)
- **Columns**: `decile` (Discrete Dimension, `1` to `10`, where `1 = Highest Predicted Risk`).
- **Rows**: `SUM([churn_rate])` (or `AVG([churn_rate])`, formatted as `0.0%`).
- **Calculated Fields**:
  - `Overall Churn Rate`: `{ FIXED : SUM([churners]) / SUM([customers]) }` (`38.5%`)
  - `Risk vs Average`:
    ```text
    IF [churn_rate] > { FIXED : SUM([churners]) / SUM([customers]) }
    THEN "Above Average Risk"
    ELSE "Below Average Risk"
    END
    ```
- **Color**: Put `Risk vs Average` on **Color** (`Above Average Risk` = Alert Coral `#D95F43` for Deciles 1–6; `Below Average Risk` = Muted Slate `#6B7280` for Deciles 7–10).
- **Reference Line**: Drag `Overall Churn Rate` to **Detail**, right-click the Y-axis → **Add Reference Line** → Value: `Overall Churn Rate (Average)`, dashed line, label: `"Avg Churn Rate: 38.5%"`.
- **Labels & Tooltip**: Show `churn_rate` (`0.0%`) above each bar (`69.8%` in Decile 1 down to `2.5%` in Decile 10). Tooltip: `Decile`, `Customers`, `Actual Churners`, `Churn Rate`, `Mean Predicted Risk`, `Cumulative Share of Churners`.

### Sheet 2B — Customers by RFM Segment (from `outputs/tableau/scored_customers.csv`)
- **Rows**: `rfm_segment` → Sort descending by `COUNTD([customer_id])` (`Champions: 576`, `Hibernating: 462`, `At Risk: 403`, `Need Attention: 340`, `Potential Loyalists: 312`, `Loyal Customers: 289`, `Can't Lose Them: 245`, `Recent Customers: 148`).
- **Columns**: `COUNTD([customer_id])`.
- **Color**: Create a clean 3-tier color pill or put `AVG([predicted_risk])` on a diverging/sequential Navy-to-Coral scale (`#1F5A9E` low risk → `#D95F43` high risk) so high-risk segments pop immediately.
- **Label**: `COUNTD([customer_id])` (plus `% of Total`).
- **Tooltip**:
  - `Segment: <rfm_segment>`
  - `Customers: <COUNTD(customer_id)>`
  - `Avg Predicted Churn Risk: <AVG(predicted_risk)>` (format `0.0%`)
  - `Actual 90d Churn Rate: <AVG(churned)>` (format `0.0%`)
  - `Avg Total Spend: <AVG(total_spend)>` (format `£#,##0`)
  - `Avg Recency: <AVG(recency_days)> days`

### Sheet 2C — Top 50 At-Risk Customers Action Table (from `outputs/tableau/scored_customers.csv`)
- **Filter**: `is_top50_at_risk` = `True` (keeps `risk_rank` `1` through `50`; predicted risk `90.9%` – `95.5%`).
- **Rows** (all Discrete, sorted by `risk_rank` Ascending):
  1. `risk_rank` (`#`)
  2. `customer_id` (`Customer ID`)
  3. `last_order_date` (**Exact Date → Discrete**, formatted `yyyy-mm-dd`)
  4. `rfm_segment` (optional, compact context)
  5. `total_spend` (or place `total_spend` and `predicted_risk` in **Measure Values** on Text so numeric columns align right cleanly!)
- **Clean Table Tip (Recommended)**: Put `risk_rank`, `customer_id`, `last_order_date` on **Rows**, put `Measure Names` on **Columns**, and `Measure Values` (`predicted_risk` formatted `0.0%`, `total_spend` formatted `£#,##0.00`) on **Text**. Add `country`, `n_orders`, `recency_days`, and `rfm_segment` to **Tooltip**.

### Dashboard 2 Assembly
- Place Sheet 2A (Decile Churn Rate) and Sheet 2B (RFM Segments) side-by-side in the upper half, and Sheet 2C (Top 50 At-Risk Customers table) across the bottom half (scrollable table for marketing export/outreach).
- Optional interactive Action Filter: enable **Use as Filter** on Sheet 2B (RFM Segments) so clicking a segment highlights/filters if desired (note: since Sheet 2C is already filtered to Top 50 overall, keep Sheet 2C unfiltered or clarify in its subtitle `"Top 50 highest-risk customers (Sept 9, 2011 snapshot)"`).

### Verified Insight Caption (Dashboard 2)
> **What the data shows:** The top two risk deciles churn at **69.8%** and **60.6%** (vs. the **38.5%** average) and capture **33.9% of all churners**, while **865 Hibernating and At-Risk buyers** alongside **245 "Can't Lose Them"** accounts (averaging **£3,937** spend and **111 days** since last order) offer the highest-impact retention targets.

## 5. Final Pre-Publish & Mobile Checklist

- [ ] **Consistent styling**: Titles on every sheet (`12–14pt`), clear subtitle noting the Sept 9, 2011 test snapshot on Dashboard 2, consistent `£` currency formatting, and max 3–4 colours across both dashboards.
- [ ] **Sorted bars & rich tooltips**: Verify Top 10 Countries, Top 10 Products, and RFM Segments are sorted descending, and tooltips use clean human-readable labels (hide unnecessary internal fields).
- [ ] **Phone-sized layout test**: In Tableau, open **Dashboard → Device Preview → Device Type: Phone** (for both dashboards) and add a **Phone** layout so KPIs stack 2×2 and charts stack vertically without horizontal squishing.
- [ ] **Publish to Tableau Public**: Click **Server / File → Save to Tableau Public** (or publish via Tableau Public Web Authoring), verify the live workbook in a browser (including mobile width), and copy the share URL.
